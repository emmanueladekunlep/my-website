The Problem

Why founders stay stuck with marketing

Most Nigerian founders are excellent at their craft — and inexperienced at marketing. That gap costs them more than they realise. These are the patterns I see in almost every mentorship conversation.

⚠

Outsourcing without understanding

You can hire someone to run marketing, but you can't evaluate their work if you don't understand marketing yourself. So you're paying for something you can't judge.

⚠

Copying tactics without strategy

Trying what worked for someone else — a competitor, an influencer, a trend — with no understanding of why it worked or whether it applies to your business.

⚠

Confusing activity with progress

Posting daily, running ads, printing flyers — plenty of activity, no measurement. You can't tell what's working because nothing was designed to be measured.

⚠

No positioning clarity

When asked what makes the business different, the answer drifts. Without clear positioning, every campaign starts on the back foot.

⚠

Fear of making the wrong decision

Marketing budgets are scarce in Nigeria. Making a wrong call feels expensive. So decisions get delayed, or made by whoever is loudest in the room — not by the owner.

⚠

Dependence on the same few people

A social media handler, a cousin who does design, one agency. If any of them disappear, marketing stalls — because the founder was never the strategic centre.

  • ✕ "I don't really know if my marketing is working."

  • ✕ "I don't know what to post or say that actually brings customers."

The Process

How mentorship begins

Simple, human, and honest. Not every enquiry becomes an engagement — and that's intentional.

Discovery conversation

A WhatsApp or call conversation about your business, your current marketing, and what you feel stuck on. No obligation, no pressure.

Fit assessment

I tell you honestly whether mentorship is the right move for you right now — or whether an agency, a course, a book or just doing the work is a better fit.

Proposal & scope

If mentorship makes sense, you receive a clear proposal: format, number of sessions, price and expected outcomes. Everything in writing.

First session — deep dive

We audit your current marketing together. Positioning, message, channels, spend, results. You leave the first session with real clarity, not just inspiration.

Implementation between sessions

You put what we discuss into practice. The mentorship works because you're executing — not just listening. This is where real learning happens.

Review, refine, repeat

Each session reviews what you implemented, what worked and what didn't. Your judgment sharpens — and marketing decisions get faster and better.

FAQ

What founders ask about marketing mentorship

What is a marketing mentor and how is it different from hiring an agency?

A marketing mentor teaches you how to market. An agency does the marketing for you. Both have value — but they serve different needs. If you hire an agency, you rent marketing expertise and lose it when the contract ends. If you work with a marketing mentor, you build marketing capability inside your business that stays with you. Many founders do both: mentor to build understanding, agency (or in-house team) to execute.

Who is marketing mentorship in Nigeria actually for?

Marketing mentorship is most useful for founders and business owners who currently outsource all marketing, in-house marketers who want to sharpen their thinking, small business owners who can't yet afford a full agency retainer, and marketing managers who want to move from tactical execution to strategic judgment. If your marketing decisions feel like guesswork, mentorship is probably the right move.

How does one-to-one marketing mentorship work?

Mentorship is structured around your business, not a generic course. Sessions happen over video calls or in person, with clear objectives per session. Between sessions, you implement what we discuss, and bring back real results to review. Depending on the engagement, I may also review your campaigns, positioning, website and messaging — so the advice is specific to your reality, not abstract theory.

How much does marketing mentorship cost in Nigeria?

Pricing depends on the engagement — a short strategy intensive, a multi-month mentorship programme, or an ongoing advisory arrangement sit at different price points. I quote individually after understanding your business and what you're trying to achieve. Every mentorship agreement is clear about scope, number of sessions and expected outcomes.

How long does it take to see results from marketing mentorship?

Founders often report the first meaningful shift after two or three sessions — usually because they finally understand why earlier campaigns weren't working. Business results follow over one to two quarters as better decisions compound. Mentorship is not a magic bullet; it accelerates learning that would otherwise take years of expensive trial and error.
