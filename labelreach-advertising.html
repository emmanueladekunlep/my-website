The Problem

Why Nigerian businesses keep getting let down by agencies

If you've hired an agency before and been disappointed, you're not alone. The Nigerian advertising space has specific patterns that produce bad outcomes — and they're predictable.

⚠

Pitched by seniors, delivered by juniors

The person who impressed you at the pitch is rarely the person doing the work. You end up paying senior rates for junior execution, with no visibility on the swap.

⚠

No measurable outcomes

Beautiful decks, but no numbers. What did the campaign actually produce? Enquiries? Sales? Foot traffic? Without measurement, "brand awareness" becomes a cover story.

⚠

Design without strategy

A designer makes what you ask for, not what would work. There's no positioning thinking, no message hierarchy, no understanding of the audience. Pretty, ineffective.

⚠

Endless vendor juggling

One vendor for design, another for print, another for digital, another for signage. You spend more time coordinating than building your business.

⚠

Deadlines treated as suggestions

Nigerian agencies have a reputation for slipping timelines — sometimes without telling you. Campaigns miss their window and you've spent the money anyway.

⚠

No post-campaign accountability

Campaign runs, invoice clears, and that's the end of the relationship. No review, no learning, no refinement. You start from zero next time.

The Process

How we work with clients

Structured, transparent, and paced so you always know what's happening next.

FAQ

What clients ask before working with LabelReach

What does LabelReach Advertising Ltd do?

LabelReach is a full-service Nigerian advertising and branding agency. We handle brand strategy and positioning, campaign design and execution, QR code advertising, NFC smart label marketing, school marketing, digital content, website and landing pages, and campaign measurement. Instead of juggling multiple vendors for a single campaign, businesses work with one partner across strategy, design and delivery.

How long has LabelReach been in business?

LabelReach Advertising Ltd was founded in 2015 by Emmanuel Adekunle Peace. That means over a decade of running real campaigns for real Nigerian businesses — not a startup experimenting on client budgets. Longevity in the Nigerian agency space is rare, and it means our advice is grounded in practical experience.

Is LabelReach a design studio or a marketing agency?

Neither label fits exactly. LabelReach is an advertising and branding company — meaning we handle both the strategic side (positioning, message, campaign planning) and the delivery side (design, print, digital builds, smart labels, tracking). We sit between a design studio that just makes things and a media agency that just buys placements — because most Nigerian businesses need both thinking and execution under one roof.

What size of business does LabelReach work with?

LabelReach works with small and medium Nigerian businesses, established companies, schools, premium brands and startups. Every engagement is quoted per project — so a small business looking for a focused campaign is welcome, and a larger organisation needing a multi-channel rollout can also be served. There's no minimum size, only a clear brief and a real budget.

How much does it cost to work with LabelReach?

Every project is quoted individually because scope varies widely — a single QR campaign, a brand identity refresh, a full school marketing programme and a multi-channel business launch sit at very different price points. We quote after a discovery conversation, and every proposal is broken into clear phases and deliverables so you know exactly what you're paying for before work begins.

Does LabelReach work with businesses outside Lagos?

Yes. LabelReach serves clients across Nigeria — Lagos, Abuja, Ibadan, Port Harcourt and beyond. Much of the work is delivered remotely, with on-site work available where the project genuinely benefits from it, such as school programmes or large format installations. WhatsApp and video calls make the process efficient regardless of location.
