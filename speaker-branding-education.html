The Problem

Why most event talks in Nigeria are forgotten by the next day

Event organisers know the pattern. The speaker is engaging, the audience applauds, and by the next week, nothing has changed. That's not a speaker problem — it's a design problem. Here's what actually happens.

⚠

Motivation without mechanism

Speaking that fires people up but gives them nothing to do differently on Monday. Inspiration without action produces applause, not change.

⚠

Generic content

The same keynote recycled for every audience — students, founders, teachers, parents all getting the same talk. Nobody feels it was meant for them.

⚠

Theory without practice

Speakers who quote research and frameworks but have never actually run a business, led a school or shipped a product. The audience senses the gap immediately.

⚠

No takeaway framework

After the talk, the audience can't summarise what they learned. If they can't repeat it, they can't use it. Great talks leave one clear framework behind.

⚠

Disconnected from Nigerian reality

Advice copied from Western content that doesn't fit the constraints, culture or economics of doing business or running a school in Nigeria.

⚠

No follow-up material

The talk ends, the speaker leaves, and there's nothing for the audience to revisit. A great event leaves behind resources — not just a memory.

The Process

How to book me

Simple, transparent and organised. From first message to post-event follow-up.

Initial enquiry

Message on WhatsApp or email with the event date, audience, topic and format. A short conversation clarifies whether it's a fit and what's realistic.

Discovery call

A 20–30 minute call to understand the audience, the theme, the outcomes you want and the tone that will land. This is where the talk begins to be shaped.

Proposal and fee

You receive a written proposal covering the topic, format, duration, fee and any travel arrangements. No surprises, no hidden costs.

Follow-up material

FAQ

What event organisers ask

What topics does Emmanuel Adekunle Peace speak on?

Emmanuel speaks across five core areas: branding and positioning for businesses and schools; marketing strategy, campaigns and modern attention; education and the future of learning in Nigeria; personal development, emotional regulation and discipline; and technology, digital transformation and coding education. Each talk is customised to the audience — a school conference gets a different treatment from a business summit.

What types of events does Emmanuel speak at in Nigeria?

Emmanuel speaks at conferences, business summits, school events, staff training sessions, church and youth programmes, book clubs, corporate workshops, education seminars and entrepreneur meetups. He is comfortable with audiences ranging from small senior leadership teams (20–30 people) to large conference halls (500+).

Can the talk be tailored to our specific audience?

Yes — and it should be. Every engagement starts with a short discovery conversation about the audience, the theme, the outcomes you want and the tone that fits. A talk to secondary school students, a branding workshop for founders and a keynote to school proprietors are three very different things, and each deserves a different approach.

What is the fee for booking Emmanuel as a speaker?

Speaking fees depend on the type of event, the location, the length of the talk and whether travel is involved. Emmanuel quotes individually after understanding the event. Some events — particularly youth programmes, non-profit education initiatives and church events — are considered for reduced or waived fees when the fit and impact justify it. Reach out on WhatsApp to discuss.

Does Emmanuel travel outside Lagos for speaking engagements?

Yes. Emmanuel speaks across Nigeria — Lagos, Abuja, Ibadan, Port Harcourt, Enugu, Kano and beyond. Travel arrangements and any associated costs are discussed during booking. Virtual talks via video call are also available for organisations that prefer that format or cannot host in person.

Can Emmanuel run a workshop or training instead of just a keynote?

Yes. Emmanuel runs longer-form workshops and training sessions (half-day, full-day or multi-day) on branding, marketing, school growth, digital transformation and personal development. These are practical, exercise-driven and designed so participants leave with real capability — not just notes. Particularly useful for school staff training and business teams.
