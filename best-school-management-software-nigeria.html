Why SkulCBT Stands Out

SkulCBT is an offline-first School Management and Computer-Based Testing (CBT) platform designed specifically for Nigerian schools. Instead of depending on constant internet access, it focuses on helping schools manage admissions, academics, finance, examinations, staff, and reporting efficiently while keeping operational costs predictable. The uploaded company profile describes features such as local deployment, integrated school administration, one-time licensing tiers, document parsing for exam creation, and security mechanisms tailored to educational records.
